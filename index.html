How it works

Seven days from first call to live website.

You talk about your business. We do the writing, the design and the launch.

Start on WhatsApp

  1. Day 1

    You tell us about your business

    A short call or a simple form. What you make or do, who buys from you, how long you have been at it. Send whatever photos you have on WhatsApp.

  2. Days 2 to 4

    We write and build your page

    AI turns your answers into a clear first draft. A person checks every line, picks the layout for your trade, and adds your logo, colours and photos.

  3. Days 5 and 6

    You review it on your own phone

    We send you a private link. Tell us what to change in a WhatsApp message. Nothing goes live until you say yes.

  4. Day 7

    We launch

    The page goes live on your web address. We test the WhatsApp button, the call button and every link before we hand it over.

  5. After launch

    Changes are one message away

    New product, new number, new photos. Send us a message and we update the page.
